# 🥁 Drum Kit Project

An interactive, web-based Drum Kit that allows users to play different drum sounds either by clicking the on-screen buttons or by pressing specific keys on their keyboard. 

This project was built to practice DOM manipulation, event listeners, and audio playback in JavaScript.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **HTML5:** For the structural layout of the drum pads.
* **CSS3:** For styling, layouts, and button press animations.
* **JavaScript (ES6):** For handling keyboard/click events and triggering audio files.

---

## 🎮 How to Play
You can play the drums in two ways:
1. **Clicking:** Use your mouse or trackpad to click directly on any of the drum buttons on the screen.
2. **Keyboard:** Press the corresponding keys on your keyboard:
   * `W` - Tom 1
   * `A` - Tom 2
   * `S` - Tom 3
   * `D` - Tom 4
   * `J` - Snare
   * `K` - Crash
   * `L` - Kick / Bass
